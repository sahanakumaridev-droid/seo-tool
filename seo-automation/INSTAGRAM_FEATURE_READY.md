## 📱 How to Use

### Step 1: Generate Content
1. Go to http://localhost:5173
2. Enter business type and locations
3. Click "Generate SEO Content"

### Step 2: Share to Instagram
1. Click "Share on Social Media" button on any page
2. In the modal, click **"📸 Instagram (Copy + Download)"**
3. Caption is copied + Image downloads automatically

### Step 3: Post to Instagram
1. Open Instagram app or instagram.com
2. Click "+" to create new post
3. Upload the downloaded image
4. Paste the caption (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V)
5. Post! 🎉

## 📸 Instagram Caption Format

```
[Business Title]

📍 [City], [State]

🔗 Link in bio

#BusinessType #CityName #LocalBusiness #SEO
```

### Example:
```
Best Plumbing in San Diego, CA | Affordable, Proven Results

📍 San Diego, California

🔗 Link in bio

#Plumbing #SanDiego #LocalBusiness #SEO
```
