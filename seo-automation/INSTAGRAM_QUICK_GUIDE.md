## 📋 Caption Format (Automatically Copied)

```
Best Plumbing in San Diego, CA | Affordable, Proven Results

📍 San Diego, California

🔗 Link in bio

#Plumbing #SanDiego #LocalBusiness #SEO
```

## 🖼️ Image Details

- **Format**: JPG
- **Size**: 1200x600px (optimized for Instagram)
- **Quality**: High-resolution
- **Filename**: `instagram-plumbing-san-diego.jpg`
- **Content**: Business-specific image (NOT random!)

## 🎬 Step-by-Step Workflow

### In Your Browser (SEO Tool):
1. ✅ Generate content
2. ✅ Click "Share on Social Media"
3. ✅ Click "📸 Instagram (Copy + Download)"
4. ✅ See success alert

### On Instagram:
5. ✅ Open Instagram app/website
6. ✅ Click "+" (Create Post)
7. ✅ Select downloaded image from your Downloads folder
8. ✅ Paste caption (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V)
9. ✅ Click "Share"
10. ✅ Done! 🎉

## ⏱️ Time Required

- **In SEO Tool**: 5 seconds (1 click)
- **On Instagram**: 25 seconds (upload + paste + post)
- **Total**: 30 seconds per post

## 🔥 Pro Tips

### Hashtag Strategy
- **Business Type**: Automatically added (e.g., #Plumbing)
- **Location**: City name without spaces (e.g., #SanDiego)
- **Generic**: #LocalBusiness #SEO
- **Add More**: You can add more hashtags after pasting!

### Image Tips
- Images are business-specific (Plumber = plumbing tools, not random)
- High quality from Unsplash
- Consistent for same business+city combination
- Professional and relevant

### Caption Tips
- Keep the heading (it's SEO-optimized!)
- Location helps local customers find you
- "Link in bio" is Instagram standard
- Hashtags increase discoverability

## 🚫 What This Does NOT Do

❌ Does NOT log into your Instagram account
❌ Does NOT post automatically
❌ Does NOT store your password
❌ Does NOT violate Instagram's Terms of Service

## 📱 Mobile vs Desktop

### Desktop (Recommended):
- Image downloads to Downloads folder

- Open Instagram in browser or app
- Upload and paste

### Mobile:
- May need to save image manually (long-press)
- Caption still copies to clipboard
- Open Instagram app
- Upload and paste
